PaperNova Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

In short: PaperNova works entirely on your device. It has no account, no cloud, no ads and no analytics, and it does not have permission to use the internet. Your documents never leave your phone unless you choose to share them.

What PaperNova stores

The photos you scan or import, the pages made from them, their names, notes and recognized text, your saved signature and your settings. All of it is stored privately inside the app on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Camera

The camera is used only while you scan. Pictures are processed on the device to find and crop the page. PaperNova never records audio or video.

Photos and files

When you add photos or open a PDF, you pick them in the system picker; PaperNova only sees the items you choose. "Save to Gallery" adds a page to your photo library only when you ask for it.

Text recognition

Text is recognized on the device (Google ML Kit on Android, Apple Vision on iOS). No image or text is sent anywhere.

Sharing

When you share, print or export (PDF, Word, images), the file goes only to the app or printer you pick. What happens next is governed by that app or service.

Data collection

PaperNova collects no personal data, shares no data with third parties and contains no tracking. We cannot see your documents.

Children

PaperNova is a general productivity app and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app and on this page, with a new date.
